## 3. Audience and the five questions the site must answer
Ship owners, operators, fleet/technical managers, superintendents, chief engineers, procurement, vessel managers, industrial/marine facility managers.
1. Can ME Group solve my technical problem?
2. Can they respond quickly?
3. Do they understand this equipment?
4. Can I trust them with a critical system?
5. How do I reach the engineering team right now?

## 5. Confirmed vs. unconfirmed

### CONFIRMED (supplied company material)
**Mechanical**: main/auxiliary machinery maintenance, pumps, generators, mechanical troubleshooting, fuel-related systems, cooling-related systems.
**Electrical power & distribution**: marine generators, AVR, main switchboards, circuit breakers, electrical distribution, earth fault detection, insulation resistance / Megger testing, thermography, AC/DC motors, bearings, starter panels, electrical troubleshooting.
**Automation & control**: PLC, control panels, monitoring systems, sensors, temperature/pressure/level monitoring, fire alarm, gas detection, AMS, PMS, generator synchronization.
**Pneumatic**: cylinders, seal kits, solenoid valves, compressors, air dryers, FRL units, pneumatic control systems, air leakage detection.
**Maintenance**: preventive maintenance, technical inspection, diagnostics, corrective intervention, rapid response.
**Vessel support**: spare parts, technical equipment, vessel provisioning (fresh/frozen food, drinking water, medical supplies), technical consumables.
**Availability**: 24/7 technical support / rapid intervention (no guaranteed response time).

### NOT CONFIRMED (never present as ME Group services)
Dry docking, hull repair, steel/piping renewal, underwater/voyage/afloat repair, blasting, coating, marine painting, vessel construction, dredging, boat lift, shaft alignment, propeller/rudder repair, tank cleaning/coating, fabrication, major machining, offshore support, ship conversion.
Architecture supports adding these later via `status: "confirmed"` content flags.

### NOT CONFIRMED (company claims)
Years of experience, project/vessel/engineer counts, ports and geographic coverage, certifications (ISO, class), OEM partnerships, clients/logos, response-time guarantees, workshop capabilities, awards, testimonials, statistics, real projects, contact details, office addresses.

## 6. Strategic patterns adopted (not copied)
Downtime reduction (Goltens), lifecycle/technical depth (Wärtsilä), structured capabilities (Damen), rapid-response conversion (Grandweld), vessel segmentation (ASRY), technical depth (Alexandria Shipyard).

## 7. Copy rules
English primary, RTL-ready. Technical, concise, operational. Banned without evidence: "world-class", "best-in-class", "leading", "unmatched", "revolutionary", "number one".
